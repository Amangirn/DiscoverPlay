Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 15, 2026

DiscoverPlay is an independent game discovery website created by AmanGirn. This page explains, in simple terms, what information the site stores and why.

Information you provide

When you create an account, DiscoverPlay stores information needed to manage your account, such as your username and email address. Your password is stored using password hashing rather than as plain text.

Favorites, Wishlist, and activity

For signed-in users, DiscoverPlay stores your Favorites and Wishlist selections so they can appear in your personal library. The site may also record game-view activity and counts to improve trending and recommendation features.

Contact messages

If you use the Contact page, the email address, message type, and message you submit are stored so DiscoverPlay can review suggestions or business-related requests.

Cookies and authentication

DiscoverPlay uses an authentication cookie to keep signed-in sessions working. It is used for account access and protected features such as Favorites, Wishlist, My Games, and the Admin Dashboard.

Third-party services

Some game images, fonts, icons, and store links may load from third-party services or websites. Those services may have their own privacy practices and terms.

Data choices

If you have a question about your account or want to request a change related to your submitted information, use the Contact page.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as DiscoverPlay gains features. The date at the top will be updated when the policy changes.
